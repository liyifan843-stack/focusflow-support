Quick Answers

The timer sound or notification is not playing

Open FocusFlow Settings and make sure sound is enabled. Then check macOS notification permissions for FocusFlow in System Settings → Notifications.

My focus statistics look incomplete

Statistics are updated when focus sessions finish. Paused, canceled, or reset sessions are not added to completed-session totals.

How do I change the timer durations?

Open Settings and adjust the focus, short-break, and long-break durations. You can also choose whether breaks and focus sessions start automatically.

How do I reset my local history?

Use the reset control in FocusFlow Settings. This removes locally stored focus statistics and streak data from your Mac.

Before Contacting Support

Include your macOS version, FocusFlow version, and a short description of what happened. If the issue involves notifications, please mention whether FocusFlow appears in macOS Notification Settings.
